# Raseed Privacy Policy

Effective date: 29 August 2026

Raseed is a privacy-first debt and loan tracker. Financial records are stored on
the user's device. Raseed does not sell personal information and does not use
financial records for advertising.

## Data handled by the app

- People, debts, loans, installments, payments, notes, and attachments entered
  by the user.
- Preferences such as language, currency, appearance, reminders, app lock, and
  widget privacy.
- Contacts selected by the user, only after permission is granted. Raseed does
  not upload the address book.
- Purchase status and store transaction evidence needed to verify or restore a
  Pro subscription. Receipt verification must use the configured secure
  verification service and must not include the user's financial records.

## Storage and sharing

The primary database is local. Optional Google Drive backup is initiated by the
user and is governed by the Google account and Google privacy terms. Portable
backups are encrypted when the recovery-key option is used. Home-screen widgets
receive only the summary required to render them and mask amounts by default.

Raseed may communicate with Apple or Google for purchases, Google for an
explicit Drive backup, and the configured receipt-verification service. It does
not disclose financial records to these services for subscription verification.

## Permissions

- Notifications: due dates, installments, recurring items, and user-selected
  reminder actions.
- Contacts: importing a person selected by the user.
- Photos/files: attachments and explicit backup import/export.
- Biometrics: local app lock; biometric material remains managed by the device.

Permission can be denied or revoked in system settings. Core manual tracking
continues without contacts, biometrics, or notifications.

## Control and retention

Users can export their data, remove individual records, disconnect optional
services, or delete all local financial data from Privacy Centre. Drive backups
must be removed separately from the user's Drive account. Store purchase
records may be retained by Apple or Google under their own policies.

## Security and children

Raseed uses platform-protected storage for sensitive local secrets and supports
privacy-safe notification and widget content. No method of storage is perfectly
secure. Raseed is a general financial utility and is not directed to children.

## Contact and policy changes

The publisher contact and hosted policy URL shown in the relevant app-store
listing are the authoritative support channels. Material policy changes will be
